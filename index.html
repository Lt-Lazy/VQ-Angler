WHAT IS VOIDQUEST?

VoidQuest is my personal collection of games, experiments and worlds.

Most projects are inspired by old-school RPGs, MMORPGs, dungeon crawlers and pixel-art games.

This launcher acts as the central hub for every VoidQuest project.
